## Decision

### Primary buyer state

The first experiment targets companies that already have a prioritized AI or automation initiative, but the operation still requires recurring rescue work and has not reached stable production.

Buyer recognition:

> Det fungerar i demon. Sedan kommer ett undantag och någon måste rädda allt igen.

This is the strongest first state because the pain is concrete, it can be recognized inside three seconds and it matches the current landing page from hero through offer, qualification and booking.

### Challenger

Do not run a separate "we need an internal AI function" challenger in the first experiment.

Hiring and internal-capacity signals remain valuable commercial evidence, but Meta cannot target that situation directly. The broader promise also needs a different hero to preserve message match. Use this buyer state in sales discovery and hold it as a future landing-page variant.

### Main falsification risk

The primary state may be too small or too technical on Meta. It may attract practitioners who recognize the problem but cannot sponsor a 25,000 to 50,000 SEK monthly relationship. The experiment must therefore be judged on held, commercially qualified conversations, not clicks or form completions.

## Paid offer bridge

### Exact painful situation

A company has already paid for software, prototypes, consultants or internal AI work. The normal path works, but missing data, changed permissions, unclear decisions and system exceptions still pull a senior person back into the loop.

The company has not removed the manual operation. It has moved the manual work into rescue, checking and coordination.

### Buyer and company context

- CEO, COO, CTO, CIO, operations leader or business-systems owner
- Swedish company with a recurring workflow that is already prioritized
- Multiple systems or teams involved
- Someone has mandate to move the workflow forward
- The problem is operationally material enough to justify a continuing build-and-operate relationship

Scale hypothesis:

- Core: 20 to 199 employees
- Allowed: 200+ employees
- Exception: 1 to 19 employees only where the operation is commercially material and multi-system

This is a hypothesis, not a ratified ICP.

### What NordSym does first

NordSym maps one real recurring workflow through:

1. Start signal and usable input
2. Systems and permissions
3. Decisions and approvals
4. Exceptions and safe stops
5. Operational evidence and measurement

The buyer receives a one-page build-or-decline decision.

### What changes operationally

The desired change is not "more AI". It is a bounded operation where:

- the normal path runs without constant supervision
- uncertain cases stop or reach the right person
- system actions are controlled
- outcomes and failures leave evidence
- one operator owns the recurring improvement loop

### Why previous attempts stall

- A prototype proves the happy path, not the operation.
- A consultant can define a roadmap without owning production.
- A tool can generate an answer without handling permissions, handoffs and exceptions.
- An internal owner can have the mandate while lacking the capacity to map, integrate, test, govern and run the entire backlog.
- Separate vendors can each own a component while no one owns the end-to-end result.

### Why NordSym is structurally different

NordSym can work as a forward-deployed builder and operating partner inside the client's existing stack. NordSym does not stop at a model, an integration or a workshop. The commercial relationship can include the governed layer around the operation and the continuing ownership required after launch.

NordSym may fill the operational function, complement an internal owner or build the working system that a future hire inherits. It does not categorically replace or merely support every internal role.

### What the free mapping is

- One real recurring workflow
- One operational boundary
- One blockage diagnosis
- One acceptance-test outline
- One system path
- One build-or-decline recommendation

### What the free mapping is not

- Not a free implementation
- Not a generic AI workshop
- Not an architecture guarantee
- Not a promise of revenue, savings or full autonomy
- Not an obligation to buy

### Commercial continuation

If the operation is viable:

1. NordSym proposes a separately approved paid build.
2. The build is tested against written acceptance criteria.
3. NordSym can remain responsible for deployment, exceptions, monitoring and continuous improvement.
4. The commercial hypothesis is a 25,000 to 50,000 SEK monthly build-and-operate relationship.

The free mapping earns the right to propose this relationship. It does not imply that every mapped workflow qualifies.

## Recording plan

### Authentic founder footage

- Record vertical 9:16 at 1080 x 1920 or 4K.
- Place the phone at eye level, approximately one arm's length away.
- Frame from mid-chest to slightly above the head.
- Face a window at roughly 45 degrees. Avoid a bright window behind you.
- Keep the home-office background real and clean. Do not build a fake studio.
- Use a lavalier microphone if available. Otherwise keep the phone close and remove room noise.
- Record each script in two versions: calm and sharper.
- Leave one clean second before and after each take.
- Do not begin with a logo animation. The first frame is Gustav's face plus the hook.

### Duration and pacing

- Target 25 to 40 seconds.
- Hook within the first two seconds.
- One sentence per breath.
- Cut every 2 to 4 seconds where it improves clarity.
- Preserve natural pauses. Do not make the delivery sound memorized.

### Subtitles

- Burn in Swedish subtitles.
- Maximum two lines at once.
- Use sentence case.
- Keep subtitles above Meta's lower interface area.
- Highlight at most one phrase per frame.

### B-roll and evidence

Authentic:

- Gustav speaking
- Hand-drawn operation map
- Redacted screenshots from NordSym-owned or approved proof surfaces
- Real architecture snippets with no client identity or private data

Can be generated:

- Abstract system nodes

- Neutral interface transitions

Must not be generated:

- Fake client dashboards

## First experiment structure

### Campaign

- Objective: Leads
- Conversion location: Website
- Destination: https://nordsym.com/ai-i-drift/
- Optimization event: Lead
- Commercial truth: held and Sales-qualified conversation, then signed profitable engagement

### Primary audience contract

Meta controls:

- Location: Sweden
- Minimum age: 25 as an initial hypothesis
- Advantage+ audience and placements
- Existing-customer and recent-converter exclusions only when lawful, accurate audience data exists

Do not set a strict language control. Swedish creative performs the language self-selection without excluding Swedish buyers who use Meta in English. Minimum age 25 remains a hypothesis that Gustav must approve, not buyer truth.

Meta cannot reliably encode:

- an active internal AI backlog
- a failed prototype
- budget for an operational AI function
- number of connected systems
- executive mandate
- active hiring as direct purchase intent

Creative and qualification must perform that filtering.

### Ad-set structure

One campaign and one broad ad set for the first round.

Do not split by job title, industry or speculative interest clusters. That would fragment a small learning budget and pretend Meta has professional targeting precision it does not have.

### Creative cells

Launch wave, subject to approval:

- Cell A: Video 1, pain diagnosis
- Cell B: Video 2, exception explanation
- Cell C: Static 1, visual recognition

Reserve iteration:

- Founder proof, trust and structural difference
- Video 3, sunk-investment recovery
- Video 4, internal-owner overload
- Carousel 1, operating-layer education

The first round uses three cells and tests concepts, not tiny copy variations. Audience, destination and offer remain constant. This is adaptive screening, not a causal A/B test, because Meta may distribute impressions unevenly.

### Campaign and cell slugs

Use stable, nonnumeric slugs:

- `utm_source=meta`
- `utm_medium=paid_social`
- `utm_campaign=ai_i_drift_e1`
- `utm_id=c01_barnvakt_v1`
- `utm_id=c02_happy_path_v1`
- `utm_id=c03_demo_drift_s1`

Use the same cell slug in the Meta ad name, Booking Engine receipt and Mission Control record. Do not rely on raw numeric Meta campaign or ad IDs because the current landing-page sanitizer intentionally drops long numeric values.

### Event contract

- PageView: consented Meta Pixel on the landing page
- Lead: completed primary-state qualification after the exact event gate below
- Schedule: server-side only after confirmed booking
- No names, emails, companies, notes or qualification answers sent to Meta
- PostHog retains the categorical funnel context

PostHog records every valid `nordsym_paid_qualification_completed` event.

For experiment one, Meta `Lead` should fire only when all conditions are true:

- `operation_state` is `active_build` or `prototype_only`
- `mandate` is `sponsor_now` or `owner_in_place`
- `systems_count` is `2`, `3-5` or `6+`
- `company_size` is `20-49`, `50-199` or `200+`

Other valid completions may still continue to booking, but they do not become Meta Leads. If this event gate is not approved, the campaign is only a message diagnostic and CPL must not be reported as qualified demand.

Do not call a Meta Lead a qualified conversation. Commercial qualification happens after the answers and held meeting are reviewed.

### Minimum decision metrics

Creative signal:

- Impressions by creative
- Three-second video view or equivalent first-frame engagement
- Outbound click-through rate
- Landing-page view

Funnel signal:

- PostHog paid landing sessions
- Qualification start
- Qualification completion
- Confirmed booking
- Held meeting
- Sales-qualified opportunity
- Proposal
- Won recurring engagement
- Realized gross margin

Meta PageView is consented-only and therefore a biased subset of visits. Use PostHog paid landing sessions as the denominator for click-to-site and on-site conversion. The first decision is not based on an industry benchmark. It asks whether one message repeatedly produces the intended buyer state.

### Kill, hold and iterate rules

Kill:

- The hook attracts job seekers, consumers or AI hobbyists rather than operating buyers.
- Message and landing page contradict each other.
- Any claim cannot be supported.
- Destination, tracking or identity is wrong.

Hold:

- The creative produces clicks but no qualification starts.
- The experiment cap is reached without enough downstream data to distinguish message failure from low volume.
- Meta delivery concentrates too heavily to compare cells at all.
- A cell has fewer than 1,500 impressions or 15 PostHog paid landing sessions. Mark it inconclusive rather than weak.

Iterate:

- After at least 1,500 impressions and 15 PostHog paid landing sessions, replace a cell if it produces zero qualification starts.
- After the same evidence floor, replace a cell if outbound CTR is below half the peer median and it produces no primary-state completion.
- Clicks reach the page but qualification completion is weak: adjust promise clarity or first-frame targeting.
- Qualification completes but bookings do not: adjust offer understanding and booking transition.
- Bookings occur but meetings are unqualified: strengthen buyer, mandate or economic language in the creative.
- Meetings qualify but no proposal follows: Sales and offer problem, not automatically a Meta problem.

Advance:

- At least two different companies produce held conversations matching the buyer state.
- At least one conversation is verified by Sales as a plausible 25,000 to 50,000 SEK monthly opportunity.

Scale:

- Only after signed recurring work and a defensible relationship between channel cost and contracted or realized gross profit.

### Lead-response workflow

1. Qualification completion is recorded in PostHog.
2. Confirmed booking creates the Booking Engine receipt and server-side Schedule event.
3. The confirmed booking creates or updates a Mission Control record keyed by booking ID and the stable `utm_id` cell slug.
4. The booking alert is reviewed with source, campaign and categorical qualification context.
5. The held call verifies the named workflow, current manual rescue, systems, mandate, urgency and commercial capacity.
6. Mission Control moves the deal through meeting done, proposal sent, won or lost.
7. NordSym Sales owns qualification and the offer.
8. CFO and Mission Control reconcile actual revenue and gross profit after a win.

There is no manual follow-up path for an anonymous form completion without a booking because the qualification intentionally collects no PII.

Report separately:

- SEK per primary-state completion
- SEK per confirmed booking
- SEK per held qualified conversation
- SEK per proposal
- SEK per won client
- Meta spend divided by realized gross profit

### Retargeting

Do not create a retargeting ad set at launch.

Add retargeting only when:

- Meta reports a deliverable consented audience
- the 30-day audience is large enough not to create immediate frequency pressure
- there is evidence that qualified visitors need an objection-handling message

Until then, retargeting fragments the experiment and adds no decision-capable learning.
